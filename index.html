ABOUT US

Learning with values and vision

Drishti Public School is located at Raghunathpur, Bhargama, Araria, Bihar. The school provides education from Nursery to Class 8.

Our focus is on quality learning, discipline, good values, confidence and the overall development of every child.

We aim to provide students with a safe, supportive and encouraging environment where they can learn and grow.

✓ Quality Education Focus on strong academic foundation.
✓ Discipline & Values Building responsible and confident students.
✓ Overall Development Education along with activities and sports.
